# Analyze and explain computational learning and adaptation models

## Documented analytical activity

The named distributional dopamine-value presentation supports personal analysis/explanation of how asymmetric prediction errors yield different value channels, why expectiles differ from quantiles and what biological evidence supports the research interpretation. It is not an original dopamine experiment or a personally implemented distributional agent.

The Team E CMAC/FFNN comparison additionally documents collaborative interpretation of local learning and generalization trade-offs, with Pablo among five authors. Individual coding and controlled universal performance advantages are not established. [Humanoid evidence](../evidence/ei7263.yaml) separates the personal presentation, collaborative comparison and taught scientific content.

## Transferable capability and educational breadth

The bounded capability is to compare a learning model's representation, target/error signal, update mechanism, assumptions and evidence. This includes recognizing the difference between a supervised motor error, a bootstrapped TD residual, an advantage-weighted policy update and a distribution of possible returns. Accredited Deep RL and humanoid-learning coursework supply substantial theoretical/algorithmic support; supplementary ADPRL/dynamics broaden context without supplying additional implementation claims.

The relevant questions are concrete: what information is assumed available, what is being approximated, which distribution weights the objective, what can be concluded from the update, and what did the experiment actually test? The canonical [value-learning](../concepts/temporal-difference-learning.md), [policy-gradient](../concepts/policy-gradient-credit-assignment.md), [local adaptation](../concepts/local-adaptive-function-approximation.md) and [distributional coding](../concepts/distributional-value-coding.md) pages preserve those distinctions.

## Scope

This supports research-method comparison, technical exposition and preparation to formulate learning problems. It does not establish individual authorship of supplied ROS/CMAC/RL code, successful CartPole training, robot deployment, neuroscience experimentation or trading performance. It complements the existing numerical-UQ analytical competency, rather than relabeling uncertainty/error-cost analysis as all learning expertise.

## Analytical breadth from mechanisms to architectures

Completed neuroscience/cognitive coursework extends the comparison vocabulary: membrane dynamics and threshold/reset, normalization versus unconstrained Hebbian growth, timing traces versus reward errors, sensory inverse inference versus output prediction, and symbolic buffers versus distributed/dynamical representations. [Spiking/plasticity](../concepts/spiking-neuron-plasticity.md), [information/inference](../concepts/information-coding-sensory-inference.md) and [cognitive architectures](../concepts/cognitive-architectures-binding.md) retain their substantive mathematics and model limits.

This enriches the documented ability to analyze/explain learning models; the personal presentation and collaborative comparison remain its attributable analytical activities. It does not create new personal neurophysiology experiments, a Brian2 implementation claim or an ACT-R software project. The theoretical knowledge remains valuable independently of those implementation boundaries.
